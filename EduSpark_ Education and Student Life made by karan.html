Student tips that work

Pick a topic.

  • ✅ Use active recall: close the book and write what you remember.
  • ✅ Space your revision: review after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days.
  • ✅ Teach a concept to a friend; if you can explain it, you know it.
  • ✅ Solve past papers under timed conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Is EduSpark free?

Yes. All tools and tips are completely free for students.

How many hours should I study daily?

Quality beats quantity. Most students do well with 3 to 5 focused hours plus regular breaks.

How do I stop procrastinating?

Start with just 5 minutes, use 25-minute focus sprints, and remove your phone from the desk.

Does my data get saved?

This demo runs in your browser and sends nothing to a server.
